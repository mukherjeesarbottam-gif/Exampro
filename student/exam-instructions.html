General Instructions

  • The examination will consist of multiple-choice questions. Each question has four options, out of which only one is correct.
  • The timer will start automatically once you click the "Start Examination" button. The test will be auto-submitted when the time is up.
  • You can navigate through questions using the question palette on the right side or the Next/Previous buttons.
  • Do not refresh the page or press the back button during the examination, as it may result in immediate submission.
  • You can mark questions for review if you are unsure of the answer and want to revisit them later.
